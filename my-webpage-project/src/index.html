About the Band

Zeynep’s Diner is a retro pop band blending the infectious melodies of the 1950s–60s with the dreamy, enigmatic atmosphere of classic American diners and the cinematic worlds of David Lynch. Their performances are a fusion of nostalgia, storytelling, and a touch of noir, inviting audiences to step into a world where every song is a scene and every gig is a story.
